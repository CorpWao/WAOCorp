Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Alexandre Allogo, publisher of the WAO Corp website (see the legal notice). You can contact us at corp.wao@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you use the quote request form, we collect the information you enter:

  • your name and email address (required)
  • your company and phone number (optional)
  • the type of project, your estimated budget and your message

We do not collect anything else through the form, and we do not use cookies or analytics tools on this website at the time of writing.

Why we use it

We use this information only to reply to your request, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, start a project together. The legal basis is the steps taken at your request before a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in answering the messages sent to us (Article 6(1)(f)).

Who receives it

  • Formspree, Inc. (United States) processes the form and delivers the message to our inbox.
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States) hosts the website and may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit.
  • Google Fonts (Google) serves the typefaces and therefore receives your IP address when the page loads.

These providers are based outside the European Union. They state that they rely on recognised safeguards for international transfers, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses; see their own privacy policies for details. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Messages and contact details are kept for up to three years after our last contact, unless a contract is signed, in which case they are kept for the legally required period.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict its use, or to object to it. Write to corp.wao@gmail.com and we will answer within one month. If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Changes

We may update this notice, for example if we add analytics or change a provider. The date at the top shows the latest version.
